About

I am a Professor in the School of Computer Science at Wuhan University. Previously, I was a Group Manager and Senior Scientist at A*STAR’s Institute of High Performance Computing in Singapore, and a Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore with Prof. Wee Sun Lee. I received my Ph.D. from Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by Prof. Yiu-ming Cheung.

My research focuses on human- and evidence-grounded AI for health and life sciences. I am interested in building models that learn from diverse biomedical data, ground their outputs in evidence, and know when they may be wrong.

My current work centers on:

  • FoundationMultimodal pretraining for medical and biomedical data.
  • GroundingConnecting predictions and generated claims to supporting visual and textual evidence.
  • TrustHallucination, uncertainty, robustness, and reliable evaluation.
