Yang Zhou 周阳

Professor, School of Computer Science, Wuhan University

VEGA Lab|eVidEnce Grounded AI

I lead the VEGA Lab at Wuhan University: (human/evidence) grounded AI for health and life science — models built so a person can check what the answer rests on.

In practice that means multimodal medical foundation models and vision–language models that tie a diagnostic claim to the image region, the report sentence, or the calibrated uncertainty behind it — and the benchmarks that expose when they cannot.

Before Wuhan I spent four years at A*STAR’s Institute of High Performance Computing in Singapore, latterly as Group Manager and Senior Scientist, and two years as a Research Fellow at NUS with Prof. Wee Sun Lee. I hold a Ph.D. from Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by Prof. Yiu-ming Cheung, and the NSFC Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas).

PhD and Master’s students, every year.

I look for solid mathematics, fluent PyTorch, and genuine curiosity about clinical problems; a medical background is welcome but not required.
