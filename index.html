About

I am a Professor in the School of Computer Science at Wuhan University, where I lead the VEGA Lab. Before Wuhan I was Group Manager and Senior Scientist at A*STAR’s Institute of High Performance Computing in Singapore, and a Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore with Prof. Wee Sun Lee. I hold a Ph.D. from Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by Prof. Yiu-ming Cheung.

My research builds (human/evidence) grounded AI for health and life science — models a clinician can hold to account:

  • Foundation ModelsMultimodal, multi-disease pretraining across fundus photography, OCT, chest X-ray and pathology.
  • Grounding & ExplainabilityTying a diagnostic claim to the image region and the report sentence that justify it.
  • Trustworthy EvaluationHallucination, calibrated uncertainty, and benchmarks that expose failure instead of hiding it.

Models, benchmarks and code are released openly — MerMED-FM, BenchX and UrFound are on GitHub.

I look for strong AI researchers — solid mathematics, fluent PyTorch — who want their models to reach real patients. No medical background needed; that side you pick up here, alongside our clinical collaborators.
