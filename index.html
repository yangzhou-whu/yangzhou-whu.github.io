About

I am a Professor in the School of Computer Science at Wuhan University. Previously, I was a Group Manager and Senior Scientist at A*STAR’s Institute of High Performance Computing in Singapore, and a Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore with Prof. Wee Sun Lee. I received my Ph.D. from Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by Prof. Yiu-ming Cheung and Prof. Haiping Lu.

My research spans machine learning, computer vision and natural language processing. I build multimodal foundation models that see, read and reason, and work to make them trustworthy: grounded in evidence, aware of when they may be wrong, and rigorously evaluated. Healthcare is our main real-world testbed, where mistakes are costly and evidence matters.

My current work centers on:

  • Multimodal foundation models: Vision–language pretraining, visual grounding, and language-guided generation.
  • LLM reasoning & agents: Multi-turn dialogue, document-level reasoning, and agents with traceable reasoning.
  • Trustworthy & explainable AI: Hallucination, uncertainty and Bayesian modelling, robustness under long-tailed data, and reliable benchmarks.
