VEGA LAB

eVidEnce-Grounded AI

VEGA is short for eVidEnce-Grounded AI. The name is the lab’s working rule: a model’s output should be traceable to evidence, and the model should say so when that evidence is missing.

Evidence is whatever a careful clinician or scientist would point to: a region of an image, a sentence in a report or a medical note, a measurement, a passage in the literature. Medical data supplies plenty of it, and most of it is never used by models that learn only from labels.

Grounded means the link is explicit. A prediction comes with the evidence that supports it, a generated sentence with the source it was drawn from, and a confidence that reflects how much support there is. Grounding is how we check a model, how a clinician can trust or overrule it, and how we find out where it fails.

In health and life sciences the cost of an unsupported answer is high, which is why we work here rather than on general-purpose benchmarks. The specific problems we are working on are on the front page.

For students

We recruit PhD and Master’s students every year and welcome undergraduates who want to start research early. Undergraduates should begin with the 本科生科研入门指南, which takes a first project from choosing a topic to a first research report in about four weeks.
