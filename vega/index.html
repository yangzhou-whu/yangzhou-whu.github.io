VEGA LAB

Verified, Grounded and Aligned AI

VEGA stands for Verified, Grounded and Aligned AI: three properties an AI system has to earn before it belongs in health and life sciences, in the order it has to earn them.

Verified. A result holds up under evaluation designed to break it: held-out patients and external cohorts, the simplest baseline that could explain the number, and reporting that keeps the failures. A model we cannot verify is a model we do not trust.

Grounded. An output is tied to evidence: a region of an image, a sentence in a report or a note, a measurement, a passage in the literature. Grounding is what lets a clinician check a claim, overrule it, and see where the model fails.

Aligned. The system serves the people who use it and the people it affects. It follows clinical intent, says when it is unsure or when the evidence is missing, and stays within the limits set by data use and patient consent.

The star Vega was the zero point of the stellar magnitude scale: every other star was measured against it. We try to build the same kind of reference points for medical AI, in models, benchmarks and evidence that others can measure against. Current projects are on the front page.

For students

We recruit PhD and Master’s students every year and welcome undergraduates who want to start research early. Undergraduates should begin with the 本科生科研入门指南, which takes a first project from choosing a topic to a first research report in about four weeks.
