VEGA Lab·Verified, Grounded and Aligned AI

VEGA Lab builds AI for health and life sciences. The name is the standard every system we build has to meet.

  • VVerified

    Tested to break, not to pass.

    • Held-out patients and external cohorts
    • Compared against the simplest baseline
    • Failures reported, not hidden
  • GGrounded

    Every answer shows its evidence.

    • An image region, a sentence in a note, a measurement or a citation
    • Evidence a clinician can check and overrule
  • AAligned

    Serves clinical intent.

    • Says when it is unsure
    • Respects data use and consent

Vega was the zero point of the stellar magnitude scale: every other star is measured against it. We want our models, benchmarks and evidence to be reference points in the same sense. Current projects are on the front page.

For students

We recruit PhD and Master’s students every year and welcome undergraduates who want to start research early. Undergraduates should begin with the 本科生科研入门指南, which takes a first project from choosing a topic to a first research report in about four weeks.
