VEGA LAB

Verified, Grounded and Aligned AI

VEGA Lab builds AI for health and life sciences. The name is the standard every system we build has to meet.

Verified. It survives evaluation built to break it: held-out patients, external cohorts, the simplest baseline that could explain the number, failures reported.

Grounded. Every output points to its evidence, whether an image region, a sentence in a note, a measurement or a passage in the literature. Evidence is what a clinician can check and overrule.

Aligned. It follows clinical intent, says when it is unsure, and stays within data use and consent.

Vega was the zero point of the stellar magnitude scale. We want our models, benchmarks and evidence to be reference points in the same sense. Current projects are on the front page.

For students

We recruit PhD and Master’s students every year and welcome undergraduates who want to start research early. Undergraduates should begin with the 本科生科研入门指南, which takes a first project from choosing a topic to a first research report in about four weeks.
