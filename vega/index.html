VEGA LAB

Human- and evidence-grounded AI for health and life sciences

VEGA Lab is Yang Zhou’s research group in the School of Computer Science at Wuhan University. We build models that learn from diverse biomedical data, ground their outputs in evidence, and know when they may be wrong.

Our current work centres on three threads:

  • Foundation Multimodal pretraining for medical and biomedical data.
  • Grounding Linking predictions and generated claims to visual and textual evidence.
  • Trustworthy Hallucination, uncertainty, robustness, and reliable evaluation.

Representative work is listed under Selected Publications.

For students

We recruit PhD and Master’s students every year and welcome undergraduates who want to start research early. Undergraduates should begin with the 本科生科研入门指南, which takes a first project from choosing a topic to a first research report in about four weeks.
